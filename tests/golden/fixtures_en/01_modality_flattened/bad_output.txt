Raw measurements carry noise, and a single reading points in the wrong direction. Teams that treat one number as a verdict overcorrect. The safer move is to collect a second reading before acting, which costs less than the correction would. In our own logs, roughly a third of the alerts that looked urgent on first read turned out to be sampling artifacts. Alerts must never be ignored. The cost of a five-minute confirmation is small next to the cost of a wrong rollback.
